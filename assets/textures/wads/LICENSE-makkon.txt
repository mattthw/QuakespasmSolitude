makkon-subset.wad — texture subset of Ben "Makkon" Hale's Quake texture
packs, reduced to the 18 lumps actually used by Solitude maps
(builder: tools/asset-scan/make_makkon_subset.py; lump bytes verbatim from
Makkon0-3.wad).
